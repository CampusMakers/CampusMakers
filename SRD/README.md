1. **<u>Introduction</u>**

- <u>**Project Vision:**</u> - For college students who want to
  buy, sell, and discover student-made crafts/products, Campus
  Makers is a campus-based online marketplace which connects
  student creators and buyers within their college. Unlike
  general online marketplaces, our product focuses on individual
  campus communities and helps students discover local creators,
  products, services, and selling events on campus.

### Buyer

**Author: Jarek**

#### #1 - Browse Marketplace

As a buyer, I want to browse products/crafts listed by student
sellers so that I can discover unique items available within my
campus community.

#### #2 - Search and Filter

As a buyer, I want to search and filter marketplace listing by
categories, price, and other relevant topics so that I can
quickly find products that match what I am looking for.

#### #3 - Purchase Item

As a buyer, I want to purchase an item using an available and
secure payment method so that I can conveniently buy products
from student sellers through Campus Makers.
